# Demo Video Script (2–4 minutes)

## Title card — first 2 seconds

> Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship  
> Level 1, Task 1 — Retail Sales EDA  
> Your Name

## Walkthrough

1. **Introduction (0:02–0:20)**
   - “Hello, I’m [Your Name]. This is my Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis project for Oasis Infobyte, Level 1 Task 1.”
   - Show the GitHub repository and this project folder.

2. **Dataset and preparation (0:20–0:50)**
   - Open the notebook’s first section.
   - Say: “I used a retail transaction dataset containing date, demographic, category, quantity, unit-price, and sales fields. I checked data types, missing values, and duplicates, then created month, quarter, and age-group fields for analysis.”

3. **Trend findings (0:50–1:30)**
   - Show the monthly and quarterly charts.
   - Say: “May is the highest-revenue month, and Q4 is the strongest quarter. This suggests planning for both the May opportunity and Q4.”

4. **Customer and product findings (1:30–2:15)**
   - Show age-group, gender, and category charts.
   - Say: “Customers aged 45–54 are the highest-revenue group, with the 25–44 range close behind. Electronics is the highest-revenue category, while gender differences are present but modest.”

5. **Relationships and recommendations (2:15–3:00)**
   - Show the correlation and age-category heatmaps.
   - Say: “Quantity and unit price are the main transaction-value drivers. My recommendations are to plan for May and Q4, lead promotions with Electronics, focus on the 25–54 segment, and test multi-unit or cross-sell offers.”

6. **Close (3:00–3:15)**
   - “The complete notebook, dataset, charts, and README are available in my GitHub repository. Thank you.”

## Recording tips

- Use 1080p if available and zoom browser text to a readable size.
- Do not scroll too quickly; pause briefly on each chart.
- Keep browser tabs free of personal information.
