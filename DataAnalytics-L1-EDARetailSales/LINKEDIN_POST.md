# LinkedIn Post Draft

Excited to share my first project from the Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship: **Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis**.

Using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn, I explored 1,000 retail transactions to uncover sales trends, customer demographics, category performance, and revenue drivers.

Key insights:
- May was the highest-revenue month and Q4 was the strongest quarter.
- Electronics generated the most revenue.
- Customers aged 45–54 were the highest-revenue group, with ages 25–44 close behind.
- Quantity and unit price were the clearest drivers of transaction value.

Based on the analysis, I recommended planning campaigns and inventory around May and Q4, using Electronics-led cross-sell offers, and testing targeted bundles for high-value customer segments.
